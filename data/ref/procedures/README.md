# Derived CIFP procedures (not for navigation)

One `<ICAO>.json.gz` per airport: STARs, runway-aligned instrument approaches, coded holds and the fixes they use.
Generated by `python3 scripts/fetch_cifp.py`; seeded into `geo.procedures` / `geo.fixes` by `scripts/db_seed.py procedures`.

- Source: FAA Coded Instrument Flight Procedures (CIFP), ARINC 424-18, cycle **2611**
- URL: https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/cifp/CIFP_261029.zip (9,113,631 bytes)
- SHA-256: `e3ff2b31a4f6ad3eda1342b83ada1ae399aae7bd8c1e67f3cacd5b288b5e141d`
- Airports: the 320 flights2015 destinations plus the 30 hubs. 318 files, about 0.8 MB.
- Header tiers: `star+iap` 110, `iap` 203, `none` 5 (PAPG, KSPS, KNYL, KVPS, KASE: no usable procedure kept).
- Skipped, no terminal data in this cycle: KISN, KPBI.
- Circling-only approaches (no runway to fly a straight-in final to) are not kept: KASE (RNV-F) and PAPG (LDA-D, RNV-B)
  get a synthetic final instead. KASE keeps its STARs but its tier reads `none` because it has no approach.

Procedures: FAA CIFP 2611, not for navigation.
